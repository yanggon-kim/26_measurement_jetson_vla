VERDICT on the E7 GPU-idle hypothesis: holds within LeRobot, fails across architectures
2026-09-24 21:15, from the complete E5 set. For reports/E5_timeline.md and E7's registered
hypothesis. This is the falsification, stated as a falsification.

## The hypothesis as registered

reports/E7_power_energy.md: the action expert's energy-to-time ratio (eager->best energy
factor divided by the latency factor) tracks its GPU-idle fraction. Registered as
falsifiable, with E5 named as the test.

## The test

Idle is the expert region's GPU-idle fraction from E5 (ae_step for the LeRobot family,
ae_total for GR00T, which invokes its action head as one unit and has no per-step range).
Ratio is E7's eager->best energy-to-time ratio.

    model         idle eager   idle best   drop     E7 ratio
    pi0.5            0.6429      0.4453    0.198      0.96
    pi0   N=10       0.8417      0.4714    0.370      0.80
    SmolVLA          0.9273      0.3795    0.548      0.45
    GR00T N1.5       0.5258      0.0008    0.525      1.05
    GR00T N1.7       0.7153      0.0008    0.715      0.88
    GR00T N1.6       0.7224      0.0010    0.721      0.66

Within the three LeRobot models the relationship is monotonic in both eager idle and drop,
which is why two points looked like support earlier this evening.

It does not survive GR00T. SmolVLA and N1.5 have almost the same drop, 0.548 against 0.525,
and ratios of 0.45 against 1.05 -- the widest separation in the whole table. N1.6 and N1.7
have nearly identical idle at both ends (0.72 -> 0.001) and ratios of 0.66 and 0.88.

VERDICT: the hypothesis is FALSIFIED as a general claim. It describes the LeRobot
flow-matching family and does not extend to the DiT action head. Do not write it up as
confirmed; write up the family-specific version with the counter-examples named.

## The finding that replaces it, and it is the stronger one

GR00T's compiled DiT action head has essentially NO GPU idle:

    N1.5 best  ae_total  wall 60.1 ms  kernel_union 60.0 ms  idle 0.0008  gap median 0.0 us
    N1.6 best            wall 73.1     kernel_union 73.0     idle 0.0010  gap median 0.0
    N1.7 best            wall 80.2     kernel_union 80.2     idle 0.0008  gap median 0.0

kernel_union equals wall to 0.1 ms: the kernels run back to back. The LeRobot experts retain
38-47 % idle after the same class of optimisation (pi0 0.4714, pi0.5 0.4453, SmolVLA 0.3795).

So CUDA graphs remove launch starvation COMPLETELY in a DiT head and only PARTLY in a
flow-matching expert. That is an architectural statement with direct bearing on the PIM
argument, and it is measured rather than inferred:

  * For GR00T's compiled head the GPU is genuinely saturated. Its bound is real work, so a
    latency win has to come from doing less work or moving data faster -- which is the
    regime a memory-side accelerator is FOR.
  * For the LeRobot experts, 38-47 % of the compiled expert region is still gap. Amdahl
    bounds what any faster memory can do there until the launches are dealt with, and
    E4i already showed the idle-time ceiling bounds faster kernels rather than fewer.

## Caveats a reader must have

* ae_step vs ae_total are not the same region. The LeRobot rows are per denoising step, the
  GR00T rows per inference. The idle FRACTION is comparable as a fraction; the wall times
  are not.
* All idle figures here are RAW. Corrected figures need each run's own profiling inflation
  factor, and reports/E5_timeline.md currently prints "—" in its corrected column for every
  row, which is a gap in the generator and not a property of the data.
* E7's ratios are eager->best over the whole action-expert sub-phase; E5's idle is the
  expert REGION. They are the same phase but measured by different tools in different runs.
