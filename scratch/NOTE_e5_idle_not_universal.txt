E5: the 84 % eager GPU-idle figure is pi0-specific, not a family property
2026-09-24 19:41, first cross-model E5 rows. Correction to carry into reports/E5_timeline.md.

                                    ae_step idle   kernels/step
    pi0     eager N=10                 0.8417          968.7
    pi0     best  N=10                 0.4714          372.7
    pi0.5   eager N=10                 0.6429         1102.7
    pi0.5   best  N=10                 0.4453          401.7

pi0's 84 % has been the motivating number for the idle-time argument and was explicitly
marked "already answered, do not re-measure". That is correct FOR pi0. It does not
generalise: pi0.5's eager ae_step is 64.3 %, twenty points lower, while running MORE
kernels per step (1102.7 vs 968.7). So the gap structure differs between two models of the
same family with the same container and the same wrap points -- a model with more launches
per step is LESS launch-starved here, which rules out "kernel count alone sets the idle
fraction".

Compiled, the two converge: 0.4714 and 0.4453. Whatever the eager difference is, it is
largely removed by compiling the three ladder callables.

## Bearing on the E7 hypothesis, with two models rather than six

reports/E7_power_energy.md registers: the action expert's energy-to-time ratio tracks its
GPU-idle fraction. Pairing E7's eager->best ratios with the idle DROP measured here:

    pi0   N=10   idle 0.8417 -> 0.4714   drop 0.370   E7 ratio 0.80
    pi0.5 N=10   idle 0.6429 -> 0.4453   drop 0.198   E7 ratio 0.96

Direction is consistent: the model whose idle falls further saves proportionally more
energy than time. But SmolVLA is the case that already looked wrong for it -- eager ae idle
84.0 % (from E11f), nearly pi0's, yet an E7 ratio of 0.45 against pi0's 0.80. Its E5 rows
are being measured now and will decide it. Two agreeing points are not support for a
hypothesis whose counter-example is already on record; do not write the hypothesis up as
confirmed on this pair.

All idle figures here are RAW. The corrected ones use each run's own profiling inflation
factor -- pi0's eager was x1.53, SmolVLA's E11f captures were x1.34-1.42, so the factor is
per-run and not a constant of the device.
