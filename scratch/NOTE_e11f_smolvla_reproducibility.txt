The pattern to carry into the report: ABSOLUTE latencies drift 3-5 % between runs an hour
apart, while the DERIVED ratios -- the two-stream slowdown and the GPU-idle fraction --
reproduce to about 1 %. That is the signature of a uniform shift (thermal or clock
residency; the gate records min==max pinned at 1300.5 MHz in all three, so it is not a
clock change) rather than a measurement defect, and it is reassuring for exactly the
quantities the PIM model consumes, which are ratios.

The profiling inflation factor is the loosest at 6.3 %, which matters because the corrected
idle fraction is computed with it. Even so the corrected idle lands within 1.2 %, because
the raw idle moves with it.

Do NOT average these three. Two of the runs are incomplete by construction and only the
third has a meta.json, a bench_commit and a gate record. Cite the spread as a
reproducibility bound and point at scratch/queues/queue_e11.log and
queue_e11_fixup.log / queue_e11_recover.log for the other two.
