MODE_50W: a clean frequency-only experiment, and what it says about energy
2026-09-24 23:20, pi0 N=10, first two points of the 50W queue. For reports/E7_*.md.

## Why this comparison is well controlled

Checked in /etc/nvpmodel.conf rather than assumed:

    mode        TPC_PG_MASK   EMC MAX_FREQ
    MAXN            0             -1  (unrestricted)
    MODE_15W      248       2133000000
    MODE_30W      240             -1
    MODE_50W        0             -1

and measured after switching:

    MAXN       16 SMs, mask 0, GPU 1300.5 MHz, CPU 2201.6 MHz
    MODE_50W   16 SMs, mask 0, GPU  816.0 MHz, CPU 1497.6 MHz

So MAXN -> MODE_50W changes GPU and CPU clocks ONLY, at constant SM count and constant
memory bandwidth. It is a frequency-only perturbation. MODE_30W will also keep EMC
unrestricted but gates TPCs (mask 240 -> 8 SMs), and MODE_15W caps EMC as well, so neither
of those is frequency-only -- state that in the report before anyone lines up all four
modes as if they were one sweep.

## Latency: sub-proportional, implying a clock-insensitive fraction

    GPU clock ratio                1.594x   (1300.5 / 816.0)
    pi0 eager  1083.19 -> 1582.89  1.461x
    pi0 best    424.68 ->  625.86  1.474x

Modelling T = C/f + M with M insensitive to clock gives M/(C+M) = 22.4 % for eager and
20.2 % for best. Since EMC is unchanged and CPU clock fell by a similar 1.470x, the
residual is work that scales with NEITHER clock: memory-bandwidth-bound time and fixed
device latencies.

This is a THIRD independent line of evidence on the memory-bound fraction, after the
roofline position (E1/E2) and the stall counters (E5b), and it is the only one that
perturbs the machine rather than observing it. It does not agree with a strong
memory-bound reading: ~20 % clock-insensitive is not a memory wall.

## Energy: lowering the clock saves much less than it seems

pi0, module rail, per inference:

                     MAXN        50W      ratio
    eager  power    34.24 W    21.09 W    0.616
           latency  1083.19    1582.89    1.461
           energy   37.09 J    33.38 J    0.900
           above-idle energy   25.00 -> 19.14 J   0.765
    best   power    42.77 W    26.16 W    0.612
           latency   424.68     625.86    1.474
           energy    18.16 J    16.37 J    0.901
           above-idle energy   13.67 -> 10.82 J   0.792

Three things to say plainly:

1. **A 37 % clock cut buys 10 % total energy and costs 47 % latency.** Energy-delay product
   is 1.32x WORSE at 50W for both modes, so if latency matters at all, MAXN wins.
2. **The above-idle saving (21-23 %) is far larger than the total (10 %)** because the frame
   takes 47 % longer and the device's baseline draw is integrated over that longer window.
   Most of the power saving is eaten by running for longer.
3. That mechanism is the same one E7's original finding described from the other side: the
   expert region is idle-dominated, so anything that lengthens wall time re-accumulates idle
   energy. Frequency scaling is the cheapest possible demonstration of it.

## Caveat

Two points of twelve. pi0.5, SmolVLA and GR00T x3 are still running. Do not generalise the
1.46x or the 0.90x until the set is complete -- SmolVLA in particular behaved unlike the
others in E7 (energy-to-time ratio 0.45 against pi0's 0.80).
