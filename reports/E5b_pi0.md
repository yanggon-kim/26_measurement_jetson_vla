## 1. The answer

**Eager**: the step takes 85.54 ms under nsys (25.1× the E6 floor of 3.40 ms) and the GPU is idle 84 % of it (969 kernels, median gap 61.6 µs). Inside the kernels, the 3 deep targets (45 % of the capture window) wait mostly on `wait` (36 % of waiting, time-weighted) and `long_scoreboard` (29 % of waiting, time-weighted); the heaviest, `ampere_sgemm_128x64_tn`, waits on `wait` 40 % at 49 % SM and 48 % L2 throughput (none of the §7 single-cause signatures). **Best**: the step takes 19.74 ms under nsys (5.8× the E6 floor of 3.40 ms) and the GPU is idle 47 % of it (373 kernels, median gap 0.1 µs). Inside the kernels, the 6 deep targets (100 % of the capture window) wait mostly on `long_scoreboard` (45 % of waiting, time-weighted) and `barrier` (15 % of waiting, time-weighted); the heaviest, `ampere_bf16_s16816gemm_bf16_128x64_ldg8_f2f_stages_64x3_tn`, waits on `long_scoreboard` 76 % at 24 % SM and 55 % L2 throughput (memory-latency-bound (long_scoreboard >= 60 % of waiting, §7)).

Sources: `results/20260922_E5_pi0_eager_N10/timeline_summary.csv`, `results/20260922_E5_pi0_eager_N4/timeline_summary.csv`, `results/20260924_E5_pi0_best_N10/timeline_summary.csv`, `results/20260924_E5_pi0_best_N4/timeline_summary.csv`, `results/20260925_E6_pi0/roofline.csv`, `results/<E5b dir>/ncu/parsed.json`. What `wait`, `long_scoreboard` etc. mean is §7's mapping (part 3).

The consolidated reading across E5/E5b/E6 (quoted from `reports/SYNTHESIS_all_measurements.md` §3.3):

* eager: expert **launch-bound**: 969 kernels/step, 62 µs gaps (4× the average kernel), instruction-cache misses, 6 % of peak BW, 15.5× floor. Prefix already efficient (compute side).
* best: launches gone (0.1 µs gaps) but the expert is **memory-latency-bound** in small bf16 GEMMs (LSB), 4.96× floor with 38 % idle left unexplained. The **prefix becomes the heavy phase**: 21.64 GB/frame moved vs 5.85 GB analytic (**3.70×**, 76 % L2 hit rate); GEMMs read ~7.4 GB of excess — Gemma MLP gate/up 2.74 GB (weight re-reads), SigLIP MLP 2.45 GB (mostly weight), Gemma down 1.72 GB (activation re-reads); weight share 0.67 (fit, indistinguishable from mixed).
