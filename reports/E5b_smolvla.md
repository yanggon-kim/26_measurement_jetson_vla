## 1. The answer

**Eager**: the step takes 93.31 ms under nsys (86.0× the E6 floor of 1.08 ms) and the GPU is idle 93 % of it (1184 kernels, median gap 64.5 µs). Inside the kernels, the 4 deep targets (72 % of the capture window) wait mostly on `long_scoreboard` (25 % of waiting, time-weighted) and `wait` (17 % of waiting, time-weighted); the heaviest, `ampere_sgemm_32x32_sliced1x4_tn`, waits on `long_scoreboard` 24 % at 41 % SM and 60 % L2 throughput (none of the §7 single-cause signatures). **Best**: the step takes 5.74 ms under nsys (5.3× the E6 floor of 1.08 ms) and the GPU is idle 38 % of it (356 kernels, median gap 0.1 µs). Inside the kernels, the 7 deep targets (100 % of the capture window) wait mostly on `long_scoreboard` (28 % of waiting, time-weighted) and `wait` (27 % of waiting, time-weighted); the heaviest, `ampere_bf16_s16816gemm_bf16_128x64_ldg8_f2f_stages_64x3_tn`, waits on `long_scoreboard` 57 % at 19 % SM and 51 % L2 throughput (none of the §7 single-cause signatures).
