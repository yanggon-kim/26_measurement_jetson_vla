## 1. The answer

**Eager**: the step takes 90.25 ms under nsys (9.8× the E6 floor of 9.25 ms) and the GPU is idle 64 % of it (1103 kernels, median gap 51.2 µs). Inside the kernels, the 6 deep targets (67 % of the capture window) wait mostly on `imc_miss` (21 % of waiting, time-weighted) and `long_scoreboard` (15 % of waiting, time-weighted); the heaviest, `CatArrayBatchedCopy_alignedK_contig__8239fae5`, waits on `not_selected` 24 % at 50 % SM and 40 % L2 throughput (compute-bound region (math_pipe_throttle > 10 %, §7)). **Best**: the step takes 21.33 ms under nsys (2.3× the E6 floor of 9.25 ms) and the GPU is idle 45 % of it (402 kernels, median gap 0.1 µs). Inside the kernels, the 12 deep targets (93 % of the capture window) wait mostly on `long_scoreboard` (45 % of waiting, time-weighted) and `imc_miss` (11 % of waiting, time-weighted); the heaviest, `triton_per_fused__softmax_add_mul_4`, waits on `long_scoreboard` 49 % at 38 % SM and 55 % L2 throughput (none of the §7 single-cause signatures).
